Resident Guide

Get ready for move-in day, find out how to use the shared spaces and know what to do if something in your room needs fixing.

Move-in checklist

  • Confirm your arrival date, check-in time and key collection details.
  • Keep your booking confirmation and photo ID handy for check-in.
  • Check which items your room already includes before you pack.
  • Pack your toiletries, chargers, study materials and bedding if needed.
  • Check your room on arrival and report any existing damage or missing items.

Using shared spaces

  • Keep the study area quiet and take calls outside the room.
  • Clear away cups, rubbish and personal belongings when you leave.
  • Keep corridors, doorways and shared walkways clear.

Reporting a maintenance issue

  1. Note your room number or the exact location of the problem.
  2. Describe the issue and when you first noticed it.
  3. Report it using the resident support details provided at check-in.
